A transformer has a moving switch. That switch is a tap changer.

On-load (OLTC) works while the transformer is live. De-energized (OCTC) only when it is off. Huaming makes both, vacuum and oil, plus the motor drive.

1. Winding taps
Several tap points on the winding.
2. Selector
Picks the next tap before the transfer.
3. Diverter / vacuum
Switches the load. Arc in oil or in a bottle.
