Lesson 01 · On-load tap changers

A transformer has a moving switch. That switch is a tap changer.

Huaming makes tap changers for transformers. On-load (OLTC) works while the transformer is live. De-energized (OCTC) only when it is off. Vacuum and oil. Plus the motor drive.

See the three parts Browse types

1 · Winding taps

The regulating winding is built with extra connections. Each connection is a tap — a different turns ratio.

2 · Selector

The selector chooses the next tap. It does not break load current. That is a quieter, slower job.

3 · Diverter / vacuum


not in oil

The diverter (or the compound switch) breaks the current. Vacuum types do that inside a bottle, so the oil stays cleaner.

Use this when…

CV2
Vacuum version of CV. In-tank compound (selector-switch). Primarily-delta transformers up to 132 kV systems. III 350 A and 600 A only. Um 40.5 / 72.5 / 126 / 145 kV.
CM2
Vacuum diverter. You want CM reliability with vacuum switching — higher current than a selector-switch, still in-tank.
SHZV
Vacuum version of CMD. Grid and heavy industrial duty.
CHVT
Converter transformers. The type commissioned at Longdong ±800 kV in May 2025.
CV · CM · CMD
Conventional oil types. Arcing still happens in oil. Full range remains in production.
WSL · WDG · ZWC
Off-circuit. The transformer must be isolated. Cage, drum, or linear.
