What you are specifying

The tap changer is the only moving assembly in the transformer tank. Vacuum types interrupt in a bottle; conventional types interrupt in oil. Compound (selector-switch) types such as CV2 combine selection and diversion in one column. Diverter types (CM2, SHZV) separate those jobs.
