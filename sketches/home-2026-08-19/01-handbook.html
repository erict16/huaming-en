What a tap changer is

§ 02 · Thirty seconds

Three lines

  1. 01 A transformer changes voltage.
  1. 02 A tap changer picks which winding tap is live, so the voltage stays in range.
  1. 03 On-load (OLTC) does this while the transformer is energised. De-energized (OCTC) only with the transformer off.
