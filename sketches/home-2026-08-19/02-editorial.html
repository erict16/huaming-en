CV2 · in-tank vacuum compound OLTC · selector-switch

The only moving part in the tank

A tap changer picks which winding tap is live so the voltage stays in range as load and system voltage move. On-load types work while the transformer is energised. Off-circuit types wait for an outage.

That is the whole job.
