# Homepage sketches — overnight-unclone (replaced)

Each file uses the matching header from `sketches/overnight-unclone/header/`. First screen is a large 3:2 contain still from `public/images/products/`. Types are CV2, CM2, SHZV, HWV, WSL, CMA7. News stays. IBM Plex Sans. Navy / steel / ink / white. Lucide-style stroke icons only.

**01 — Compact bar.** Steals Hitachi Energy’s isolated product on a light plate, then a six-cell family strip (MR “latest products” *rhythm*, not their swiper). News is a dated list. Title, PDFs, 4–6 weeks, Shanghai, Singapore are on the first scroll.

**02 — Navy bar.** Steals Siemens’ painted identity bar and a data ribbon under the photograph. CM2 still is 3:2 contain on navy (screen blend). Types are a single horizontal strip. News is a numbered list, not a card carousel.

**03 — Steel rail.** Steals ABB’s document-forward table: type / kind / PDF under a large HWV still. News is two dated lines. A buyer can reach a datasheet without a campaign slide.

**04 — Left lockup.** Steals MR homepage *order* only: big still, then type strip, then news. No gradient caption, no gold, no mega menu. SHZV is 3:2 contain, full width of the pane.

**05 — Ink bar.** Steals Hitachi Energy’s specification strip (lead time, factory, HQ, PDFs) under the still. Types as a six-up row. News as two lines. Built for an export buyer who wants facts before atmosphere.
