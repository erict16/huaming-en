# Huaming English site — six plans

Marketing chair. These are six different sites, not six tints of navy and gold. Vote only after this file exists. Do not vote in this file.

Recommended repo slug default: `huaming-en`. Use a stronger slug only if the winning stance needs it (`huaming-types`, `huaming-field`, `huaming-intl`).

## Who this site is for

Primary: an overseas buyer, utility engineer, or transformer OEM who needs a type, a PDF, and a contact. English may be their second language. Easy understanding is the top copy rule.

Secondary: investor, journalist, job candidate.

This site replaces https://www.intl-huaming.com/ (Joomla, stiff, machine-translated). It is not Huaming Hub and not the OLTC selector. Link out to https://erict16.github.io/huaming-hub/ and https://erict16.github.io/oltc-selector/.

English only. No prices. No country coefficients. No Qu-ET.

## The six plans

1. **Plan 01 — Utility handbook** (`docs/plans/plan-01.md`) — The site is a printed utility catalogue that happens to load in a browser. Cream paper, type plates, PDF first. Slug: `huaming-en`. Name: Huaming.

2. **Plan 02 — Industrial editorial** (`docs/plans/plan-02.md`) — The site is a T&D house magazine: one object, large type, almost no cards. Slug: `huaming-en`. Name: Huaming.

3. **Plan 03 — Buyer console** (`docs/plans/plan-03.md`) — The site is a parts console: find a type, download the PDF, mail Singapore. Dark, dense, no hero photo. Slug: `huaming-types`. Name: Huaming Types.

4. **Plan 04 — Field story** (`docs/plans/plan-04.md`) — The site is a commissioning ledger: country, HVDC, and offshore first; products second. Slug: `huaming-field`. Name: Huaming Field.

5. **Plan 05 — Explainer-first** (`docs/plans/plan-05.md`) — The site teaches what a tap changer is in plain English, then sells the range. Course energy, not a children’s comic. Slug: `huaming-en`. Name: Huaming.

6. **Plan 06 — Singapore HQ / global maker** (`docs/plans/plan-06.md`) — The site is an international company with two headquarters, not a Shanghai factory microsite. Slug: `huaming-intl`. Name: Huaming International.

## What we refuse to copy from MR / Reinhausen

- The red system, the mark, and any asset from reinhausen.com.
- Their type names used as our teaching vocabulary.
- Their product photography, diagrams, and brochure layout.
- House copy that sounds like theirs.
- Competitor comparison pages that name them.

Also refuse from our own live site: “Global Leader in Solar Products” as a home claim, Edison/Tesla pastiche, “Free / Product details” shop tiles, fake map pins, animated vanity counters.

Home, learn, products (OLTC, OCTC, accessories, each type), downloads, about / history / why, projects, renewable, news, careers, contact, investors, privacy, imprint.
