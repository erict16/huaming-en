# Marketing ballot — 2026-08-19

**Role:** Marketing

Primary reader: overseas buyer, utility engineer, transformer OEM. English is often L2. Easy understanding beats brand theatre.

Positioning we want: Huaming makes the moving switch inside a power transformer — on-load and de-energized, vacuum and oil — and you can name a type, take the PDF, and mail Singapore in one sitting. Not a solar EPC. Not a Shanghai factory shop. Not a Reinhausen shade.

Refuse from MR / Reinhausen: the red system and mark, their product names as teaching terms, their slogan voice (“reimagine voltage regulation”), any layout that reads as a German-incumbent clone.

IA we want: home teaches the object; header skips to types, downloads, contact. Learn is a real page, not a ghetto. Products are families then types, not a 27-tile shop. Projects and Singapore sit after the device, not on top of it.

## Ranked top 3

1. **05** — The first viewport *is* the homepage story: one plain sentence names the device, the next splits OLTC / OCTC, and a known buyer can skip to types without sitting through a lesson.
2. **01** — Closest honest industrial claim (“tap changers for power transformers”) and the market already buys from brochures, but the cover still assumes the reader already knows the noun.
3. **02** — Strongest peer-OEM cover (one object, huge type, no Joomla tiles) without copying MR red, but a photograph plus a metaphor is slower for L2 and for anyone who came for a PDF.

## Veto

**04** — Bounce: the first line is a place-name headline a buyer in Lagos or Recife cannot decode, and the stance repeats the live site’s category error — projects and energy stories in front of the device they came to specify. Thin sourced jobs would collapse the home; even a full ledger would still make the wrong object the cover.
