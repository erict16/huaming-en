# Vote — UI/UX

Date: 2026-08-19  
Role: UI/UX  
Independent ballot. Plans only. Not other votes.

Industrial B2B OEM. Buyers come for a type and a PDF, not a landing. Refuse Inter-on-glow, hero kicker pills, generic purple SaaS, MR red system.

## Ranked top 3

1. **01 — Utility handbook.** Paper, ink, one oxide-red CTA: a printed catalogue plate, not a marketing composition. Ownable industrial surface. Tables beat tiles.

2. **02 — Industrial editorial.** True white, one full-bleed device, huge type in negative space. Hitachi/Siemens Energy cover craft. No cream, no pill, no wash.

3. **05 — Explainer-first.** Paper plus one diagram yellow on a 3-step CSS/SVG. Designed object, not a stock crop. Course-home is the tax; the diagram is not.

## Veto

**06 — Singapore HQ / global maker.** Buyer bounce and a template. Cool grey, Neue Haas, Tai Seng still: holding-company / airline home. They came for CV2 and get an office. Products-in-the-header does not save a first viewport that is not the product.

(04 is the near-miss: night-blue photo-essay is the energy-hero kit, and it is unbuildable if Longdong is the only real still. One veto; 06 is cleaner.)

## Strongest first-viewport object

**02’s full-bleed CV2 or HWV photograph**, type sitting in real negative space, no overlay, no kicker. That is the object they sell, at cover scale.

01’s cream cutaway-plus-folio is the most *designed* plate. It is a system, not a stronger object.

## Mobile risk of #1 (≤640)

The plate plus folio, two CTAs, and the 3-line tap-changer strip will stack past one screen; the cutaway becomes a stamp or it pushes “Find a type” below the fold. Serif H1 must drop hard or it widows into poster type. Later tables (the actual product) need a stacked definition list, not a sideways-scroll catalogue, or 01 reads as “open this on a laptop.”

## Fonts I would actually ship

Not Roboto. Not Inter. Not Geist/Satoshi.

- **Source Serif 4** — titles, captions, the plate (variable; printed, not magazine-costume).
- **IBM Plex Sans** — nav, UI, tables; tabular figures for Iᵤ and kV.
- **IBM Plex Mono** — type strings only (`CV2III-350Y/40.5`).

If 02 wins the room: same pair; do not add Newsreader on top. One serif, one sans, one mono for designations.
