

CM Type CM is a classic on-load tap changer composed by diverter switch & tap selector. It covers the widest application range from small power up to large power transformers. After a long history of operation and modifications, today type CM is one of the most reliable tap changers, which is able to withstand heavy duty with over 1,000 daily operations in furnace application.
