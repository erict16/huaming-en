Commercial Leader and Key Account Manager
Location: Singapore
Department:
Commercial Team
Reports to:
Regional Sales Manager / Senior Management
Job Summary:
The Commercial Leader and Key Account Manager will be responsible for defining and executing market entry strategies, developing go-to-market roadmaps, and managing strategic projects to achieve sales targets. This role requires strong leadership, market analysis, and relationship-building skills to establish and maintain strong connections with key customers. The candidate will also be responsible for setting up and managing a high-performing sales team.
Key Responsibilities:
Analyze the market to identify opportunities and challenges in the South East Asia region.
Develop and implement market entry strategies and go-to-market roadmaps.
Define project-based strategies in collaboration with field sales to secure strategic orders.
Establish and maintain strong relationships with key customer accounts.
Set sales budgets, pricing strategies, and ensure achievement of sales targets.
Build and manage a sales team as per business requirements.
Provide leadership and guidance to the sales team to achieve sales objectives.
Prepare and present sales reports, forecasts, and market analysis to senior management.
Collaborate with the engineering, product development, and marketing teams to align strategies.
Qualifications:
Bachelor’s degree High Voltage Electrical Engineering, or a related field.
Minimum of 7-10 years of experience in a commercial leadership role within the power industry or High Voltage equipment industry.
Proven track record of developing and implementing successful market strategies.
Strong leadership skills with experience in building and managing sales teams.
Excellent communication, negotiation, and relationship-building skills.
Ability to analyze market trends and develop actionable insights.
Strong understanding of High Voltage equipment and power transformer market dynamics.
Willingness to travel extensively within the Southeast Asia region.
Preferred Qualifications:
MBA or equivalent advanced degree.
Experience with on-load tap changers and power transformers.
Familiarity with the regulatory and business environment in Southeast Asia.

Ability to inspire and lead a sales team towards achieving strategic goals.

Proven ability to develop and implement market entry and go-to-market strategies.

Excellent verbal and written communication skills.

Effective stakeholder management and collaboration skills.

Proven ability to set sales budgets and pricing strategies.

Direct Field Sales Representative
Location: Singapore
Department:
Commercial Team
Reports to:
Regional Sales Manager
Job Purpose:
The Direct Field Sales Representative will market our range of on-load tap changers for power transformers. This role requires a strong technical background, particularly in High-Voltage Electrical Engineering or Mechanical Engineering with electrical knowledge. The candidate will work closely with utility customers and High-Voltage equipment producers to address technical specifications and commercial terms.
Key Duties:
Market and sell on-load tap changers for power transformers to new and existing South East Asian customers.
Develop and maintain strong relationships with key stakeholders, including utility companies, power generation firms, and equipment producers.
Provide technical expertise to customers, addressing their questions regarding product specifications and commercial terms.
Conduct product presentations, demonstrations, and training sessions for customers.
Identify and pursue new business opportunities to expand market share.
Prepare and submit sales reports, forecasts, and market analysis to the Regional Sales Manager.
Collaborate with the engineering and product development teams to meet customer requirements.
Requirements:
Bachelor's degree in High Voltage Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, or a related field.
Minimum of 3-5 years of commercial experience with high-voltage equipment producers or utility customers.
Proven track record of sales achievements in the power generation industry.
Strong technical knowledge of High Voltage equipment and on-load tap changers.
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
Ability to work independently and as part of a team.
Willingness to travel extensively within the Southeast Asia region.
Desired Qualifications:
Experience within a Power Generation company.
Knowledge of regional market dynamics and customer needs in South East Asia.
Cultural Sensitivity:
Understanding of cultural differences and ability to work effectively in a diverse environment.
