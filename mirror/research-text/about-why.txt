WHY US
Excellence in Tap Changers
As tap changer is a key component of power & distribution transformer and critical asset of end user, superior quality and reliability is the utmost important reason for Huaming’s growth in the last three decades. We create values for our customer by short lead time, faster commercial & technical response, full product range, extended warranty, global service network, in addition to most competitive pricing.

Full Product Range
Having launched a number of “world-first of its kind”, Huaming today has a most complete tap changer product ranges, including vacuum type, non-vacuum type, dry type, gas-insulated type, in-tank & compartment type, etc. No matter what requirement you have for voltage regulation, there is always a solution for you.

02
Short Delivery Lead Time
As Huaming has an in-depth vertically-integrated manufacturing process, it has a lot of flexibility and control on its commitment for short delivery time. The standard lead time is about 4~6 weeks. In urgent cases, it can be further reduced upon inquiry.
03
Extended Warranty
Having confidence in its product quality and reliability, Huaming today offers the longest standard warranty period in the industry.
04
Fast Response Time
We’re very proud of our high efficiency in providing commercial & technical response to our customers. With a most dedicated, experienced and stable team, we will always be standing by, whenever you need it.
05
Global After-sales Support
With products running in over 140 countries, Huaming set up a worldwide network of after-sales support. Our global service centers provide spare parts, training as well as on-site service by well trained technicians. We’re also open to certify and cooperate with third parties for service provision.
06
Competitive Pricing
We provide affordable product and service solutions for better value to our customers. Life cycle cost is even more competitive. Huaming, Your Smart Choice.
CERTIFICATE
Certificate
Huaming has passed certification of ISO-9001, ISO14001 and ISO 18001 management systems of quality, environment and occupational health. In addition to it, our type test center has passed accreditation as per IEC/ISO 17025.
PRODUCTS & SOLUTION
Products & Solution
Huaming provides the most complete voltage regulation solutions to all types of customers, including transmission and distribution grid, power generation, metallurgical plant, railway, chemical industry, oil & gas, as well as various special applications.
