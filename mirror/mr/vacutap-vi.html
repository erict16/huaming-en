VACUTAP® VI

Less is more: Cut Resources. Boost Performance.

The VACUTAP® VI was specifically developed for grid operation and limited space conditions. Thanks to its intelligent design, direct drive and a shared oil supply with the transformer, the VACUTAP® VI ensures reliable performance while reducing material, installation and maintenance costs. The sustainable on-load tap-changer is suitable for compact new transformer designs as well as for retrofitting existing oil tap-changers.

Your Advantages at a Glance

  1. Shared oil supply with the transformer and direct drive: reduction of components

  2. Flat contact alignment combined on one side

    Flat contact alignment combined on one side: simplified winding connection, faster installation and commissioning

  3. Material savings: smaller transformer with the same output

  4. Automated checks: fail-safe switching – even during power outages

  5. Vacuum interrupters: ultra-fast arc quenching

  6. Automatic drive control with two options for flexible installation

  7. Fully compatible with AVR and SCADA systems

  8. Sustainability-focused – lower CO₂ emissions over its lifecycle
