WHY US

Excellence in Tap Changers

As tap changer is a key component of power & distribution transformer and critical asset of end user, superior quality and reliability is the utmost important reason for Huaming’s growth in the last three decades. We create values for our customer by short lead time, faster commercial & technical response, full product range, extended warranty, global service network, in addition to most competitive pricing.

Full Product Range

Having launched a number of “world-first of its kind”, Huaming today has a most complete tap changer product ranges, including vacuum type, non-vacuum type, dry type, gas-insulated type, in-tank & compartment type, etc. No matter what requirement you have for voltage regulation, there is always a solution for you.
